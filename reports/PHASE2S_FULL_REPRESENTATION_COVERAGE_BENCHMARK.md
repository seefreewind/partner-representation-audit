## Technical failure architecture

Univariable effects summarize technical failure versus success. Multivariable logistic models use at most three prespecified technical predictors and require at least10 minority events per variable; nonidentifiable/separated fits are withheld. Disease identity and phenotype association statistics never enter a model. Variant and pair summaries are descriptive and do not assume independent variants within a gene. Results are in failure_predictor_models.tsv and failure_stage_descriptive_effects.tsv.
