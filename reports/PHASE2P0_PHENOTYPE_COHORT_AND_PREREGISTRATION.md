## Frozen features and controls

For primary ready genes, X-PAIR and SPPIDER matrices use identical residue IDs and alphabetically frozen named common partners. Raw sorting is verified to preserve each row score multiset exactly. Mean, max, population SD and fraction≥0.5 controls are stored without any disease-specific score summary. Exact probabilities are taken from existing WT tracks; no neural inference was rerun. The common-partner median is 17; median retained group size is 16.5 residues.

Position, domain proxy, generic interface and generic 3D are frozen with explicit missing masks. The available domain files are UniProtKB features, not Pfam/InterPro; the formal Pfam/InterPro baseline is unavailable, and the protocol separates the frozen proxy. Structural missingness is retained. The primary endpoint remains labeled-versus-sorted partner identity.

## Preregistered statistical plan

Primary X-PAIR; confirmatory SPPIDER; primary Euclidean distance on within-gene column-standardized vectors; independent unit gene×residue. Primary endpoint is prospective ΔR² labeled−sorted, with paired label permutations preserving exact group sizes. B=10,000 or exhaustive smaller assignment space; separate BH-FDR families for labeled association and ΔR². Global summary resamples genes 10,000 times. Support requires positive median with CI excluding zero and ≥70% genes with positive ΔR². Sensitivities, constant-dimension handling, complete-case baseline comparisons, restricted permutation strata, secondary prediction rules and overlap interpretation are fully frozen in the specification. None of these tests has been executed.
